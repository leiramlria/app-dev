# My Favorite Movie

## Project Hail Mary
Based on Andy Weir's 2021 book, **Project Hail Mary** is a science fiction movie from 2026 that was written by Drew Goddard and produced and directed by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller. Ryan Gosling, who also served as the film's producer, plays Ryland Grace, while Lionel Boyce, James Ortiz, and Sandra Hüller play supporting parts. The story centers on **Ryland Grace**, *a middle school teacher who wakes up on an interstellar spacecraft and has no idea how he got there.*

### Plot
> In the near future, **Dr. Ryland Grace**, a former molecular biologist and middle school teacher, awakens from an induced coma on board the interstellar spacecraft Hail Mary with amnesia. He finds out that he is the only member of a three-person crew that is traveling 11.9 light-years from Earth to the Tau Ceti system.
>
> In flashbacks, Grace recalls that scientists discovered a "Petrova line" of infrared light between the Sun and Venus. A dark substance in the line, dubbed "astrophage", was proliferating on the Sun, dimming it at a rate that would cause catastrophic global cooling within thirty years. Eva Stratt, head of the Petrova Taskforce, recruited Grace because of his expertise in speculative astrobiology. Grace discovered that astrophage samples consisted of unicellular organisms capable of absorbing electromagnetic radiation from the Sun and expelling it for propulsion.
>
> Grace joined Stratt's Project Hail Mary, an international "long-shot" mission that used astrophage as fuel to send a crewed spacecraft to Tau Ceti. The only nearby star that wasn't obscured by astrophage was Tau Ceti. Since the spacecraft could only carry enough fuel for a one-way trip and space probes would have to return any findings, the mission was a suicide mission.
>
> In the present, Grace comes upon an alien spacecraft that docks alongside the Hail Mary. Constructed from a solid form of xenon that Grace refers to as "xenonite," it transports a five-legged, rock-like extraterrestrial from Erid, a planet in the 40 Eridani A system. Grace gives the alien the name "Rocky," concludes that Eridians use echolocation to sense their surroundings, and creates a machine translation system to decipher Rocky's musical language. As the only survivor of the Eridians' quest to prevent astrophage from destroying their own star, Rocky is a mechanical engineer. After agreeing to work together, Rocky and Grace quickly grow close. Rocky and Grace build pressurized xenonite chambers inside the Hail Mary for Rocky to work in because neither can withstand the other's atmosphere.
>
> Grace and Rocky investigate the Petrova line around Tau Ceti e, which Grace names "Adrian" after the name he gave to Rocky's Eridian mate.[b] They discover a microbial biosphere in the line; Rocky theorises that other organisms further down in Adrian's atmosphere keep the astrophage population in check. After learning that Grace cannot return home, Rocky offers to share some of his astrophage fuel with the Hail Mary. Grace then remembers that an astrophage explosion killed the original crew's science officers, and that Stratt recruited him when no replacements could be trained in time.
>
> During a risky maneuver to collect the astrophage's predator, a fuel leak causes the Hail Mary to spin uncontrollably, knocking Grace unconscious. Rocky enters Grace's atmosphere to save him, but is badly injured. While Rocky recovers, Grace studies the captured predator, names it "taumoeba"[c] and selectively breeds it to survive in the nitrogen-rich atmosphere of Venus. After Rocky recovers, the pair celebrate their success. Grace then remembers that he initially refused to join the mission, but Stratt had him drugged and put aboard the ship, believing he was the only person capable of completing it.
>
> Rocky and Grace split up to go back home. Grace soon learns that the taumoeba has gotten out of its xenonite breeding tanks and started eating the astrophage fuel from the Hail Mary. Despite containing the outbreak, he realizes that Rocky's xenonite ship is unable to contain the organism and will eventually run out of fuel, leaving Rocky stranded and Erid doomed. In order to save Rocky and the Eridians, Grace decides to give up his remaining fuel and send his taumoeba samples and video logs to Earth via probe.
>
> Stratt and her team receive the probes, and use the taumoeba to cure the Sun's astrophage infection. On Erid, Grace lives in an Earth-like biodome built for him by the Eridians. Rocky tells him that Eridian scientists can prepare the Hail Mary for a return voyage to Earth. Grace considers this before beginning another day teaching science to enthusiastic Eridian children.
